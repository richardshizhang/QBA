# QBA Projects

Tools built for QBA, the Quantitative Biology Association, USC's student club for undergraduates in the Quantitative Biology (QBIO) major. Each project lives in its own folder.

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [course-dependency-graph](course-dependency-graph/) · [**open the map**](https://richardshizhang.github.io/QBA/course-dependency-graph/) | Interactive prerequisite map of the QBIO BS for planning course order. It covers AP credit, requirement waivers and offering history. |
| [faculty-map](faculty-map/) · [**open the map**](https://richardshizhang.github.io/QBA/faculty-map/) | Interactive map of USC QCB faculty by research similarity, with lines between people who have published together. Search by person or topic, or describe your interests to find your closest faculty. |

These are unofficial student-made tools. Always confirm academic plans with a QBIO advisor and your STARS report.
